# Storfirman — Product Strategy & Phased Roadmap

> Working product strategy for differentiating **Firman** (5,990 SEK/month) from **Storfirman** (11,990 SEK/month), with an initial 2–3 month focus on making Firman excellent before actively selling Storfirman to larger companies.

## 1. Strategic thesis

Storfirman should **not** feel like "Firman with higher AI limits".

The product distinction should reflect a real change in how a company operates as it grows.

**Firman helps you run the work.**  
**Storfirman helps you lead and scale the company.**

A smaller company can often be coordinated directly by the owner. As the organization grows toward roughly 7–25 employees, complexity increases: more people can make decisions, several teams/work leaders emerge, approvals become bottlenecks, management needs better visibility, and the owner can no longer keep every job and exception in their head.

Storfirman should solve that organizational complexity.

---

## 2. Recommended sequencing

### Phase 1 — First 2–3 months: win with Firman

Primary commercial/product focus should be **Firman**.

Objectives:

- make core workflows excellent
- reduce onboarding friction
- improve activation/time-to-value
- validate customer behavior with real companies
- improve reliability and polish
- develop the AI experience
- collect data on how companies actually use Handymate
- learn where owners begin to experience management bottlenecks

During this phase, Storfirman capabilities can be designed and selectively developed, but Handymate should avoid prematurely building a broad enterprise feature set without evidence.

### Phase 2 — Build the Storfirman foundation

Use learning from Firman customers to build the organizational layer required by larger companies.

Priority areas:

1. Organization, teams and work leaders
2. Roles and advanced permissions
3. Delegated approvals / approval rules
4. Approval Center
5. Management by Exception
6. Management dashboard
7. AI management reporting
8. Audit trail

### Phase 3 — Sell Storfirman deliberately

Once the core Storfirman management features are credible, actively target larger businesses, initially around the segment where organizational complexity becomes painful (roughly 7–25 employees, while validating the actual threshold from sales data).

The upgrade/sales argument should be based on **control, delegation and management leverage**, not merely employee count.

# 3. Product positioning

## Firman — 5,990 SEK/month

**Drive the business efficiently.**

Firman should contain an excellent version of the core operating product:

- jobs
- customers
- planning
- employees/users
- documentation
- core financial workflows
- normal reporting
- AI assistance
- standard product support
- Academy/onboarding when introduced

Do not intentionally cripple important everyday functionality in Firman to force upgrades.

## Storfirman — 11,990 SEK/month

**Lead and scale the business.**

Everything in Firman, plus capabilities designed for organizational complexity:

- substantially higher AI usage
- teams / organizational structure
- work leaders
- advanced roles and permissions
- delegated approvals
- configurable approval rules
- Approval Center
- management by exception
- advanced management dashboard
- AI management reports
- deeper profitability/team analysis
- audit trail and approval history
- potentially advanced integrations/API capabilities
- potentially priority support

The customer should upgrade because their company has developed a management problem that Storfirman solves — not because Handymate arbitrarily charges more after employee number seven.

# 4. Storfirman product pillars

## Pillar 1 — Organization & Teams

Handymate should understand the customer's organization, not only its list of users.

Example:

### Service Team
- 6 people
- Work leader: Johan

### Contracting Team
- 9 people
- Work leader: Lisa

### Administration
- 3 people

This structure can become the foundation for:

- job visibility
- planning
- permissions
- approvals
- notifications
- reporting
- dashboards
- KPIs

The CEO/owner can see the whole organization. A work leader can see and manage their team. A field employee sees what is relevant to them.

---

## Pillar 2 — Delegated Approvals

This is a high-priority Storfirman capability.

In a small company, the owner can approve everything. In a 15–25 person company, this becomes a management bottleneck.

Storfirman should allow approval authority to be delegated by role, team, project and monetary threshold.

Example:

- **CEO** — approves everything above 50,000 SEK
- **Work leader** — approves activity for Team North up to 25,000 SEK
- **Project manager** — approves within own projects up to 10,000 SEK

Potential rules:

- purchase <10k → work leader
- 10–50k → project/project-area responsible
- >50k → CEO

The exact approval objects should be driven by actual Handymate workflows, potentially including:

- time reports
- purchases
- change/additional work (ÄTA)
- invoice preparation
- leave
- quotations above thresholds

### Core value

The owner should no longer be the bottleneck for routine decisions.

---

## Pillar 3 — Approval Center

Create one place for everything awaiting a decision.

Example:

### Needs your approval

- Time reports — 12
- Purchases — 4
- ÄTA — 3
- Invoice preparation — 7
- Leave — 2
- Quotes above threshold — 1

The system should eventually use AI/rules to separate normal items from anomalies:

> 23 items look normal  
> 6 should be reviewed

Potential action:

**Approve all without deviations**

The value proposition is not simply digitizing approvals. It is reducing the number of decisions management must personally inspect.

---

## Pillar 4 — Management by Exception

This is a candidate **killer feature** for Storfirman.

Management should not have to inspect every job. Handymate should continuously identify which parts of the business require attention.

Example management view:

### Good morning, Andreas

47 active jobs. **5 need your attention.**

- 🔴 Project Sjövägen — 18% over budget
- 🟠 Villa Andersson — unusually high material cost
- 🟠 Project Eken — no activity for 6 days
- 🔴 3 time reports missing approval
- 🟡 Customer Nilsson — invoice basis ready for 9 days

**Handle deviations →**

This creates an important AI differentiation:

**Firman gets an AI assistant.**  
**Storfirman gets an AI management system.**

AI usage limits can still be higher in Storfirman, but the more defensible value is what the AI does with organization-wide context.

---

## Pillar 5 — Management Dashboard

The Storfirman dashboard should answer:

> **How is the company doing, and where do I need to act?**

Potential metrics where supported by reliable underlying data:

- revenue / revenue forecast
- gross margin
- utilization / billable utilization
- active jobs
- jobs with deviations
- completed but uninvoiced work
- average time from completed job to invoice
- capacity utilization
- budget vs actual by job
- team performance

Example team view:

| Metric | Service | Contracting |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Active jobs | 27 | 8 |
| Utilization | 82% | 76% |
| Deviations | 2 | 4 |
| Forecast | ↑ | ↓ |

Avoid dashboards consisting of charts simply because data exists. Every major element should help management make a decision.

---

## Pillar 6 — AI Management Report

A recurring management summary can create substantial perceived value without requiring management to log in constantly.

Example:

### Your week with Handymate

**Going well**  
Service Team utilization increased from 76% to 83%.

**Needs attention**  
Three projects are behind plan. Project Eken represents the largest financial risk.

**Cash-flow opportunity**  
184,000 SEK of completed work is waiting for invoicing.

**Next week**  
Capacity is 94% booked Tuesday–Wednesday. Friday is only 61% booked.

**Handymate recommendation**  
Consider moving two smaller service jobs to Friday.

All AI-generated financial/operational conclusions must be grounded in actual customer data, with clear handling of uncertainty. Avoid invented savings or unsupported claims.

This could later be delivered in-app and/or through appropriate notification channels.

---

## Pillar 7 — Advanced Roles & Permissions

Storfirman should support more sophisticated responsibility boundaries than Firman.

Potential dimensions:

- role
- team
- project
- financial visibility
- approval rights
- customer visibility
- administrative capabilities

Examples:

- owner sees everything
- work leader manages their team
- project manager sees financial data only for their projects
- field employee sees relevant operational information
- administrator manages users without gaining unnecessary financial authority

Permissions should remain understandable. Avoid creating an enterprise IAM system unless customers genuinely require it.

---

## Pillar 8 — Audit Trail

As more people gain authority, accountability becomes more important.

Storfirman should provide a reliable history such as:

- Johan changed the price
- Lisa approved the change
- Marcus changed reported hours
- Johan reopened the job
- Andreas approved invoice preparation

The audit trail can cover:

- who
- what
- when
- previous/new values where appropriate
- approval history

This is less visible in marketing but increasingly valuable as customer organizations grow.

# 5. Possible secondary differentiators

These may become Storfirman capabilities but should be validated before being treated as commitments:

### Advanced integrations / API

Larger organizations tend to have more systems and more complex integration requirements. Storfirman may eventually include broader API/integration access.

### Priority support

Could be included as a commercial benefit, but should not mean deliberately poor support for Firman customers.

### Advanced profitability analysis

Potential deeper analysis by:

- project
- team
- work type
- customer
- work leader

Only expose metrics that can be calculated reliably from the data available in Handymate.

### Automated reports

Scheduled reports to owners/work leaders may be particularly useful for larger organizations.

---

# 6. Upgrade motion

Handymate should detect **organizational pain**, not simply count employees.

Signals that a Firman customer may need Storfirman could include:

- many approvals handled by one owner
- multiple informal work leaders
- growing user count
- large number of active jobs
- high management notification volume
- frequent need to filter by teams
- multiple people requiring different financial access
- increasing approval delays

Example contextual upgrade message:

> **You personally approved 73 items last month.**  
> With Storfirman, Johan and Lisa could have handled 61 of them using delegated approval rules.
>
> **See how delegation works →**

This is preferable to:

> You now have 8 employees. Upgrade to Storfirman.

The first message demonstrates an actual problem and quantifies the potential management leverage.

# 7. Suggested initial priority order

When Storfirman development begins in earnest, recommended sequence:

### P0 — Foundation

1. Organization/team data model
2. Roles and permission foundation
3. Work-leader responsibility model

These unlock much of the rest.

### P1 — Delegation

4. Delegated approvals
5. Approval rules / monetary thresholds
6. Approval Center
7. Approval history

This solves an immediate management pain.

### P2 — Management intelligence

8. Exception detection
9. Management by Exception view
10. AI management summary
11. Management dashboard

Start with a small number of trustworthy exceptions rather than trying to make AI analyze everything.

### P3 — Scale/enterprise polish

12. Advanced audit trail
13. Scheduled management reporting
14. deeper profitability/team reporting
15. integration/API enhancements where demand exists
16. additional enterprise controls discovered through larger customers

# 8. What not to do

Avoid creating artificial pain in Firman simply to make Storfirman look better.

Do not unnecessarily paywall:

- basic product quality
- normal product support
- basic reporting
- core onboarding/Academy
- everyday functionality required to get value from Handymate

Storfirman should command a higher price because it solves **different and more expensive management problems**.

Also avoid prematurely building features associated with generic enterprise software unless Handymate customers demonstrate a need for them.

---

# 9. Validation during the first 2–3 months

While focusing commercially on Firman, actively learn what should become Storfirman.

Questions for customers:

- Who currently approves purchases, time, invoice bases and changes?
- What still has to go through the owner?
- At what company size did this become painful?
- Do you have formal or informal work leaders?
- What information can work leaders see today?
- What does the owner check every morning/week?
- What surprises management negatively at the end of a job/month?
- Which problems do you discover too late?
- What reports are manually assembled today?
- What would you delegate if you trusted the system to enforce limits?

Instrument Handymate so actual usage can answer the same questions where possible.

---

# 10. Pricing hypothesis

Current working price points:

- **Firman: 5,990 SEK/month**
- **Storfirman: 11,990 SEK/month**

The near-2x price can be justified if Storfirman materially reduces management overhead and gives larger companies better control.

The value case should eventually be expressed through outcomes such as:

- fewer approvals requiring owner involvement
- faster approval turnaround
- fewer unmanaged deviations
- shorter time from completed work to invoicing
- better visibility across teams/projects
- management time redirected from routine checking to exceptions

Do not rely solely on "more users" or "more AI" to justify the price difference.

---

# 11. North-star experience

The ideal Storfirman customer experience is:

> **I no longer need to know everything happening in the company. Handymate makes sure the right person handles normal work and brings me the things that actually need my attention.**

That should guide product decisions more strongly than an arbitrary checklist of enterprise features.
