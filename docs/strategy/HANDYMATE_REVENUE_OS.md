# Handymate Revenue OS — Founder-led GTM, seller system & traction strategy

> Status: Canonical GTM / internal revenue operating-system strategy
> Date: 2026-09-12
> Primary goal: Get the first 7–10 paying customers fast, prove a repeatable path to 20–30, and build the internal system that Andreas, Christopher and future sellers use to scale toward 200+ customers.

## 1. North Star

Handymate should not build a generic CRM where a seller opens a blank company card and decides what to do next.

The internal Revenue OS should continuously answer:

> **Which company should this seller work now, why now, who should they contact, what should they say, and what did we learn from the outcome?**

The system combines market signals, account research, deterministic scoring, seller actions, conversation outcomes and product/customer data into a closed learning loop.

```text
MARKET / SIGNALS
  -> BUYER RADAR
  -> ACCOUNT BRIEF
  -> SELLER QUEUE
  -> OUTREACH / MEETING
  -> RESPONSE / OUTCOME
  -> LEARNING LAYER
  -> BETTER SCORING + BETTER PLAYBOOKS
```

Astra/AI is the intelligence layer. Handymate remains the system of record for accounts, evidence, actions, stages and learning.

---

## 2. Three operating modes

### Founder mode
Andreas and Christopher need market-wide visibility: best accounts, active experiments, pipeline risk, objections, partner channels and what is converting.

### Seller mode
A future seller should receive a prioritized daily queue with:
- why this account matters now;
- evidence/signals;
- likely pain hypothesis;
- best contact/persona;
- recommended channel;
- recommended hook;
- relevant case/playbook;
- explicit next action.

### Manager mode
Leadership should see conversion by signal, segment, salesperson, channel, playbook and offer, plus stale deals and learning from closed-won/closed-lost.

**Binding UX rule:** no seller should have to start from an empty CRM record.

---

## 3. Customer milestones and what each phase must prove

### 0 → 7–10 customers: founder-led proof
Goal: prove that a narrow ICP will pay 5,990 SEK/month (preferably Annual 59,900 SEK) for Handymate and reach meaningful value.

Optimize for learning and speed, not automation volume. High-touch onboarding is acceptable.

### 10 → 30 customers: repeatability + investor proof
Goal: prove that acquisition is becoming a system rather than founder intuition.

Evidence should include:
- paying customer count;
- ARR / annual cash collected;
- annual-plan share;
- activation rate and time-to-value;
- usage/engagement;
- retention/churn;
- quantified customer outcome where credible;
- testimonials/case studies;
- qualified pipeline;
- funnel conversion;
- sales-cycle length;
- which signals/channels predict wins;
- support/implementation burden;
- expansion interest (Accounting / Storfirman).

Investor narrative target:

> **Capital is not needed to discover whether anybody wants Handymate. Capital accelerates a motion that already converts, retains and can be handed from founders to sellers.**

A 10% financing should therefore be considered only against traction and a clear use of funds, not as a substitute for validating the motion.

### 30 → 200 customers: scale proven channels
Goal: hire sellers/marketing into a documented machine, preserve activation and retention, and expand channels such as partners/referrals/content/outbound.

## 4. Buyer Radar

Every account receives a transparent score with component-level rationale. Do not use a single opaque AI score as financial/product truth.

Initial 100-point model:

```text
ICP fit              0–25
Pain likelihood      0–20
Timing / trigger     0–20
Growth signal        0–15
Warmth               0–10
Ability to pay       0–10
--------------------------
Total                0–100
```

The weights are hypotheses. Revenue OS must later measure whether each component actually predicts meetings and closed-won.

### Initial ICP signals

High-value signals may include:
- Swedish trade/service business;
- ~3–20 employees initially;
- electrical, plumbing/HVAC, construction/service and adjacent trades;
- owner/CEO still operational;
- growing revenue or headcount;
- recruiting tradespeople;
- recruiting administrator/coordinator/project manager (very strong pain signal);
- new branch/location;
- visible project volume;
- active website/social/reviews;
- likely Fortnox/manual admin stack;
- recently won larger project/tender;
- partner/referral introduction;
- explicit admin pain from conversation.

Signals must store source/provenance and observed date. AI may summarize evidence but must not fabricate it.

## 5. Account Brief

For every priority account, Revenue OS should prepare:

```text
Why now?
What evidence supports that?
Likely admin pains to test (not assert as facts)
Best persona/contact
Recommended first channel
Personalization hook
Relevant Handymate use case/case study
Recommended CTA
Recommended next action
```

Separate **facts** from **hypotheses** visibly.

Example:

```text
FACT: Recruiting two electricians.
FACT: Revenue grew materially in latest available period.
HYPOTHESIS: Owner may be experiencing increasing quote/project/invoice admin.
RECOMMENDED HOOK: “You look like you are in the phase where admin starts growing faster than the team.”
```

## 6. Seller Queue

The primary operational surface is not the database; it is **Today’s Queue**.

Queue priority should combine:
- account score;
- signal recency/strength;
- due/overdue next action;
- active opportunity stage;
- prior engagement/warmth;
- stale-deal rescue.

Target output:

```text
1. Nordström El — 88 — hiring + growth — CALL TODAY
2. Firma B — 84 — accountant referral — WARM INTRO
3. Firma C — 80 — admin job ad — CALL + EMAIL
...
```

A seller should be able to execute, log outcome and create the next action from the same surface.

---

## 7. Sales wedge: Admin Leak Audit

The preferred early CTA is not generic “book a demo”.

Use a **20-minute Admin Leak Audit** to inspect:
- lead intake;
- missed calls;
- quote speed/follow-up;
- project handoffs;
- time/material capture;
- invoicing;
- reminders/collections;
- owner/admin hours.

Deliver a short diagnosis:

```text
Top admin leaks
Estimated burden (only if evidence supports it)
What Handymate can automate now
Recommended first workflow to offload
```

The audit doubles as discovery and creates structured sales-session data.

---

## 8. First-customer offer: “We build Handymate around your business”

For the first customers, high-touch implementation is a feature, not a failure to scale.

The early offer can include:
- org/business setup;
- Fortnox/integration setup;
- initial customer/project/workflow setup;
- Karin configuration;
- quote/invoice workflow;
- selected automation;
- first live workflow verified.

The learning objective is to turn repeated implementation work into future product/onboarding automation.

---

## 9. Trigger outbound

Outbound should be event-driven whenever possible.

Examples:

```text
Hiring tradespeople            -> growth/timing trigger
Hiring admin/coordinator       -> strong admin-pain trigger
New annual report / growth     -> capacity trigger
New office/branch              -> complexity trigger
Large project/tender           -> operational-load trigger
Partner referral               -> warmth trigger
Old lead gets new trigger      -> reactivation trigger
```

The goal is not “more personalized spam”. It is **better timing**.

- insurance brokers;

## 11. Playbooks

Initial playbooks:

```text
Growing 5–10 person trade company
Owner drowning in admin
Hiring administrator/coordinator
Fortnox-heavy/manual workflow
Missed leads / weak follow-up
Accountant/referral introduction
Storfirman
```

Each playbook defines:
- trigger/evidence;
- pain hypotheses;
- discovery questions;
- recommended demo path;
- proof/case;
- objections;
- offer;
- cadence/next actions.

Playbooks are versioned. Closed-won/lost learning updates them rather than living in individual sellers’ heads.

## 12. Astra / Revenue Agent responsibilities

AI should help with:
- account research;
- source-backed brief generation;
- prioritization suggestions;
- personalized hooks;
- call prep;
- follow-up drafts;
- transcript summarization;
- objection extraction;
- stale-deal detection;
- identifying patterns in closed-won/lost;
- suggesting score/playbook changes;
- surfacing old accounts with fresh triggers.

AI must **not initially**:
- auto-send outbound without human approval;
- silently change opportunity stage;
- invent signals, financials or personal details;
- create questionable inferred personal contact data;
- decide bespoke discounts/contract terms;
- mark an account qualified based only on model intuition.

The model recommends. The durable Revenue OS records facts and approved actions.

## 13. Revenue Karin — internal daily interface

Target examples:

> “12 new high-priority accounts since yesterday.”

> “3 old leads have fresh hiring/growth triggers.”

> “Christopher has 4 active deals with no next action.”

> “Implementation-time is the fastest-growing objection this week.”

> “Electrical companies with a hiring trigger are converting materially better than generic construction accounts.”

This should become exception/action oriented, similar to the customer-facing Karin philosophy.

## 19. Investor traction case at 20–30 customers

The strongest early investor package should make the funnel auditable.

Dashboard/data-room evidence:

```text
Paying customers
MRR / ARR-equivalent
Cash collected upfront
Annual-plan share
Logo retention / churn
Activation rate
Median time-to-value
Weekly/monthly engagement
Quantified outcomes with methodology
NPS/testimonials/case studies
Qualified pipeline
Lead -> meeting -> demo -> won funnel
Sales-cycle length
Channel/signal performance
Implementation/support burden
Gross-margin / COGS evidence where available
Expansion demand: Accounting / Storfirman
```

At 30 Core customers at 5,990 SEK/month, annualized subscription value is ~2.16 MSEK before future expansion. The more important proof is whether those customers are active/retained and whether the acquisition motion is becoming repeatable.

### Fundraise framing

Weak:
> “Give us capital so we can try marketing.”

Strong:
> “We proved a narrow ICP, a high-value offer and a repeatable sales motion. Founder-led acquisition is working; the Revenue OS captures the playbook. Capital buys seller/marketing capacity and product acceleration without losing the learning loop.”

Any proposed dilution/valuation remains a financing decision, not a Revenue OS assumption.

## 20. Privacy and evidence rules

- Treat email/contact data as personal data where applicable.
- Store provenance for external signals.
- Do not infer sensitive personal attributes.
- Prefer business-context signals over intrusive personal profiling.
- Support correction/deletion where required.
- Keep outbound human-reviewed initially.
- Respect channel/provider terms and applicable marketing/privacy rules.

## 22. Definition of success for V1

V1 is successful when both founders can use the same system daily to answer:

1. Which 10–20 accounts should I work today?
2. Why are they prioritized?
3. What is the best next action?
4. What happened last time?
5. Which accounts/deals are stale?
6. Which signals and objections are appearing repeatedly?
7. Can a future seller understand and execute the playbook without founder oral history?

---

## 23. Final rule

> **Revenue OS is not a CRM database and Astra is not an email generator. Revenue OS is Handymate's internal learning-and-execution system for turning market signals into retained customers, then turning every sales outcome into a better next decision.**
