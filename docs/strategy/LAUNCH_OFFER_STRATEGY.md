# Handymate — Launch Offer Strategy

> Status: Commercial launch strategy / working decision document
> Date: 2026-09-11
> Scope: Launch packaging, annual-first sales, Founding offer, risk reversal, future Accounting bonus, price protection and Storfirman positioning.
>
> This document captures the current commercial thesis. Exact guarantee language, future module pricing and contractual terms must be validated before public use.

## 1. Core launch thesis

Handymate should **not launch by discounting the core monthly price**.

The goal is to preserve the price anchor while making annual commitment disproportionately attractive through:

- upfront savings;
- implementation/onboarding value;
- risk reversal;
- Founding-customer advantages;
- future product upside;
- price protection.

The commercial model is:

> **Monthly is the flexible way to buy the product. Annual is the launch offer.**

The offer should sell the value of Handymate as it exists at launch. Future Accounting/Financial OS capabilities are upside/bonuses, not required to justify today's purchase.

Do not make monthly artificially bad. Monthly is the risk-reducing option.

### Monthly / Flex

```text
Handymate
5,990 SEK/month

- Full current core product
- Monthly flexibility
- Standard onboarding
```

### Founding Annual

```text
Handymate Founding Annual
59,900 SEK/year

- Save 11,980 SEK vs monthly
- Done-with-you onboarding
- Defined migration/setup assistance
- Founding Price Protection
- Launch risk reversal
- Accounting Founding Access
```

This makes the choice psychologically and economically clear:

- Monthly optimizes flexibility.
- Annual optimizes total value.

The annual offer should win because the customer expects to use Handymate for more than ten months and receives benefits that materially reduce implementation risk.

## 5. Do not discount 5,990 at launch

Avoid launch tactics such as:

```text
5,990 -> 3,990 for the first year
20–30% permanent launch discount
lifetime discount on everything Handymate ever builds
```

Reasons:

- damages the price anchor;
- creates low-price cohorts that are hard to migrate later;
- signals uncertainty about the product's value;
- reduces future ARPU before value-expansion modules even exist.

Prefer increasing **offer value** over decreasing **product price**.

## 6. Hormozi-style value equation application

The launch offer should improve all four dimensions of the value equation.

### Dream Outcome — increase

Primary outcome:

> Handymate handles the administration around running a trades business so the owner can focus on customers, employees and profitable work.

Long-term direction can be communicated carefully:

> Handymate is being built toward becoming the operating system for the trades — including deeper financial automation over time.

Do not make future roadmap functionality necessary to justify the purchase.

### Perceived Likelihood — increase

Use implementation support and concrete proof:

- Done-with-you onboarding;
- Fortnox connection;
- setup of Karin and relevant workflows;
- first real customer/project/invoice flow verified;
- testimonials/case studies as soon as real evidence exists.

The strongest early promise may be less about another feature and more about:

> **We help you get Handymate working in your actual business.**

### Time Delay — decrease

Aim for a concrete activation outcome such as:

> Live and operational quickly after onboarding.

Do not publicly promise a fixed timeframe (e.g. 48 hours) unless operations can consistently deliver it.

### Effort & Sacrifice — decrease

The target customer should not feel that buying Handymate creates a new IT implementation project.

Reduce effort through:

- guided configuration;
- migration/import help;
- integration setup;
- workflow templates;
- proactive support during activation.

## 7. Accounting as a Founding bonus

Accounting can be a powerful annual-plan bonus, but it must be communicated honestly because it is not yet a generally available product.

### Recommended positioning

> **Handymate Accounting — Founding Access**
>
> When Handymate Accounting becomes generally available, eligible Founding customers receive the Accounting module without an additional module fee for a defined introductory period.

The exact introductory period must be defined before the offer is sold. A strong working option is:

```text
12 months from general availability
```

Alternative structures may be evaluated, but avoid ambiguous promises such as "Accounting included forever".

### Do not claim an invented monetary value

Do not market:

> "Accounting value 2,000–3,000 SEK/month"

until Handymate Accounting has a real scope and genuine standalone/list price.

Once the module is actually priced, its real value can be shown transparently.

Example only:

```text
Handymate Accounting list price: 2,490/month
Founding Accounting Access: 0/month for first 12 months after GA
Actual bonus value: 29,880 SEK
```

The number above is illustrative, **not a pricing decision**.

### Do not sell the roadmap

The core launch pitch should not become:

```text
Accounting is coming
Pay is coming
Payroll is coming
Supply is coming
Capital is coming
...
```

Sell today's Handymate on today's value. Accounting is a meaningful Founding upside.

## 8. Founding Price Protection

Price protection can create a strong reason to commit early because Handymate is expected to become substantially more valuable as additional verticals are added.

Recommended principle:

> Protect the customer's **Core** price for a defined period while the annual subscription remains continuously active.

Working example:

```text
Founding Core Price Protection: up to 3 years
```

Exact duration and contract wording remain a commercial decision.

### Critical boundary

Price protection must **not** mean:

> Every future Handymate product/module/service is included forever at today's price.

It should apply to the defined Core package.

Future products may have their own economics:

```text
Accounting       module/tier pricing after Founding bonus
Pay              transaction/payment economics
Payroll          module/tier pricing
Supply           supplier/procurement economics
Capital          partner/financing economics
Insurance        commission/partner economics
Marketplace      transaction/lead economics
```

This preserves future expansion ARPU.

## 9. Risk reversal is critical for annual-first sales

At 59,900 SEK upfront (and especially 119,900 SEK for Storfirman), the primary annual-plan objection may be risk rather than absolute price:

> "What if we pay for a year and it doesn't work for our company?"

A defined risk-reversal mechanism can materially increase annual conversion.

### Working concept

A launch guarantee / evaluation period, potentially around 60 days, where a customer who has genuinely completed onboarding and attempted to implement Handymate has a defined exit path if the product does not fit.

The final mechanism must specify:

- eligibility;
- required onboarding/usage steps;
- notification deadline;
- refund/cancellation mechanics;
- exclusions/abuse protection;
- treatment of implementation/professional services;
- B2B contractual/legal wording.

Do not publish a vague "money-back guarantee" before these rules are finalized.

The commercial purpose is simple:

```text
59,900 SEK + perceived lock-in risk
```

should become:

```text
59,900 SEK + annual benefits + a credible way to verify fit
```

## 10. Done-with-you onboarding as a high-value bonus

For Handymate's customer segment, implementation friction may be a larger purchase barrier than missing product features.

Potential Founding onboarding scope:

- business/account setup;
- Fortnox integration;
- core customer/project setup;
- Karin configuration;
- invoice flow configuration;
- workflow/automation setup;
- import/migration help within clearly defined limits;
- verification of first live workflow.

Do not assign fake "bonus value" to onboarding. If a monetary value is communicated, it should correspond to a service Handymate genuinely sells or would sell at that price.

Operational capacity must be considered before promising white-glove onboarding to a large cohort.

At approximately 120k SEK ARR, Storfirman should be treated as a higher-touch B2B sale rather than merely a larger self-serve checkout.

Recommended motion:

```text
Demo / discovery
  -> business workflow mapping
  -> commercial proposal
  -> annual agreement
  -> implementation/onboarding plan
  -> activation
```

Potential Storfirman differentiation should be based on genuine needs such as:

- more users/teams;
- permissions/roles;
- multiple operational units;
- deeper onboarding/migration;
- higher support expectations;
- advanced reporting/controls;
- future finance/accounting depth.

Do not invent artificial feature restrictions solely to force customers upward.

## 13. Future pricing after Accounting + Pay

When Financial Kernel, Ledger and Pay mature, Handymate's commercial position changes from an administration product toward a business operating system.

Future conceptual packaging may look like:

```text
Handymate Core
  operations / CRM / sales / projects / invoices / Karin

Handymate Business
  Core + Accounting + Bank/Reconciliation + deeper finance

Handymate Complete / Storfirman
  deeper controls / payroll / advanced financial automation / larger organizations

Embedded services
  Pay / Capital / Supply / Insurance
```

Exact packaging is deliberately **not decided here**.

The pricing principle is:

> Handymate's ARPU can rise while the customer's total cost of ownership falls because Handymate replaces other software and administrative labor.

This is preferable to pricing new modules only based on Handymate's infrastructure cost.

## 16. Scarcity and urgency rules

If using "Founding 100", the cap must be genuine and trackable.

Do not use fake countdown timers, false capacity claims or an endlessly extended "launch price ends Friday".

Legitimate urgency can come from:

- limited Founding cohort;
- finite white-glove onboarding capacity;
- defined Founding Price Protection eligibility;
- defined Accounting Founding Access eligibility.

If the cohort fills, the communicated Founding offer must actually change or close.

Agents must not silently invent answers to these commercial decisions.
