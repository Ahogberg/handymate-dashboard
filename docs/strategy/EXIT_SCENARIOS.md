# Handymate Exit Scenarios

**Status:** Strategic working document  
**Date:** 2026-08-09

## 1. Purpose

This document captures the current strategic exit thinking for Handymate.

It is not a valuation, forecast or promise. It is a scenario framework for thinking about what Handymate could become, what kinds of outcomes could support different strategic values, and what capabilities would need to exist for those outcomes to be credible.

## 2. Core thesis

Handymate has the potential to become much more than a CRM for trade companies.

The strongest long-term category is:

> A vertical AI operating system for trade and construction companies that helps them sell, plan, execute, document and get paid more effectively.

The highest-value version of Handymate would not merely store business data. It would actively improve economic outcomes by:

- improving quote follow-up and win rates
- identifying missed revenue
- protecting project margin
- improving billing speed
- reducing owner/admin workload
- coordinating specialist agents across workflows
- learning from quote-to-outcome data

The stronger the evidence that Handymate changes customer economics, the less it should be valued like a generic CRM.

## 3. Scenario framework

### Scenario A — Strong vertical SaaS

**Profile:**
- strong Swedish niche position
- approximately 10–20 MSEK ARR
- solid retention
- reliable quote/project/invoice workflows
- AI features increase usefulness but are not yet a major moat

**Indicative exit range:**

> **50–150 MSEK**

This would represent a good vertical SaaS outcome, but not the full strategic potential of Handymate.

### Scenario B — Nordic category leader

**Profile:**
- approximately 30–60 MSEK ARR
- strong retention and customer economics
- meaningful Nordic expansion
- Handymate becomes operationally important to customers
- agents and automation are trusted parts of daily work
- real Revenue Recovery / margin protection evidence exists
- proprietary outcome data begins to compound

**Indicative exit range:**

> **200–500 MSEK**

At this stage, strategic buyers could value Handymate above ordinary CRM multiples because the platform owns a deeper position in customer operations.

### Scenario C — AI operating system winner

**Profile:**
- approximately 75–150+ MSEK ARR
- Nordic or broader European position
- strong net retention
- agents actively execute or coordinate meaningful work
- Handymate can prove measurable customer economic value
- Offer-to-Reality learning is trustworthy
- proprietary outcome intelligence improves pricing, planning and execution
- Handymate becomes deeply embedded in project and revenue workflows

**Indicative exit range:**

> **700 MSEK–1.5+ billion SEK**

This is the scenario where Handymate stops being viewed primarily as software for tradespeople and starts being viewed as infrastructure for how trade businesses operate.

## 4. Internal strategic target

A reasonable internal strategic ambition is:

> **Build toward a 500 MSEK+ exit case while deliberately preserving the possibility of 1 billion SEK+**

This does not mean optimizing for a near-term sale.

It means building the product, data model and market position so that such an outcome becomes possible if the company executes well.

## 5. What creates exit premium

The most valuable Handymate is not the version with the most features.

The exit premium is likely to come from the following assets.

### 5.1 Mission-critical workflow position

Handymate should sit inside:

```text
Lead
→ Quote
→ Accepted work
→ Project
→ Time / material / ÄTA
→ Completion
→ Invoice
→ Payment
→ Outcome
```

The more of this lifecycle Handymate truthfully owns, the more difficult it becomes to replace.

### 5.2 Revenue Recovery

If Handymate can reliably identify and help recover missed billable value, its value proposition becomes directly economic.

Examples:

- finished but uninvoiced work
- missed ÄTA
- stalled quotes
- overdue receivables
- margin leakage

### 5.3 Offer-to-Reality intelligence

A particularly strong long-term moat is the ability to learn from:

```text
What was quoted
→ What actually happened
→ What it actually cost
→ What was invoiced
→ What margin was realized
```

If sufficiently trustworthy, this can improve:

- future pricing
- labor estimates
- material assumptions
- project planning
- risk detection

### 5.4 Outcome data

The strongest proprietary data is not generic CRM data.

It is outcome-linked operational data such as:

- quote accepted/rejected
- estimated vs actual labor
- estimated vs actual material
- expected vs realized margin
- detected revenue leakage
- intervention
- eventual recovered value

### 5.5 Agent orchestration

If Matte and specialist agents become a genuinely useful operating interface, Handymate can move beyond classic menu-driven software.

A long-term interface model may become:

> Tell Matte what you need done. Matte coordinates the business system.

This can be especially valuable for a non-technical trade-company audience.

## 6. Why international expansion matters

A Swedish-only Handymate can still become valuable.

However, the strongest billion-SEK outcome becomes much more credible if Handymate expands beyond Sweden.

Potential sequence:

```text
Sweden
→ Nordic countries
→ selected European trade markets
```

International expansion increases:

- TAM
- strategic buyer interest
- ARR ceiling
- dataset diversity
- category-leadership potential

## 7. Valuation logic

For planning purposes, Handymate should not anchor on one fixed SaaS multiple.

A strong vertical SaaS business may receive a moderate ARR multiple.

A strategic AI operating system with:

- high growth
- low churn
- high NRR
- proprietary data
- mission-critical workflows
- proven economic outcomes

can justify materially more.

Illustrative reasoning:

```text
50 MSEK ARR
× 5–8x
= 250–400 MSEK
```

A more exceptional company may trade materially above this.

The goal is therefore not merely ARR growth.

The goal is to create the conditions that justify a premium multiple.

## 8. What would support a 500 MSEK+ outcome

Examples of evidence that would materially strengthen such a case:

- 30–60+ MSEK ARR
- strong year-over-year growth
- low churn
- high activation
- increasing product depth across core workflows
- reliable finance/project truth
- repeatable customer ROI stories
- meaningful agent usage
- measurable Revenue Recovery
- proprietary outcome dataset
- early Nordic expansion

## 9. What would support a 1 billion SEK+ outcome

Likely additional requirements:

- 75–150+ MSEK ARR or exceptional strategic value
- expansion beyond Sweden
- category leadership
- very strong retention
- strong product moat
- meaningful outcome intelligence
- deep operational integration
- platform-level value beyond simple CRM
- strategic buyer competition

## 10. Likely buyer logic

Potential strategic acquirers could value Handymate for different reasons.

Possible buyer categories include:

- accounting / ERP platforms
- construction software companies
- field-service platforms
- Nordic business-software groups
- payroll / finance platforms
- PE-backed vertical software consolidators
- international trade-contractor SaaS platforms

The strongest buyer story is likely:

> Handymate owns the operating layer between sales, projects, field execution and payment for trade companies, and its data makes the system increasingly intelligent over time.

## 11. What would reduce exit value

Key risks include:

- broad feature set without strong retention
- unreliable project/invoice truth
- AI that feels cosmetic
- weak customer adoption
- low trust in automations
- high service burden
- poor tenant/security foundations
- pricing too low relative to value
- no proprietary data moat
- Swedish-only position with weak growth

## 12. Strategic principle

Handymate should not optimize for being sold early.

The better strategy is:

> Build a strong cash-generating vertical AI company whose economics and proprietary intelligence create multiple credible exit options.

If the company reaches 30–50 MSEK ARR with strong retention and value proof, selling immediately may not be optimal if the product still has a realistic path toward significantly larger strategic value.

## 14. Current internal ambition

> **Base strategic target:** 500 MSEK+ possible exit case

> **Upside design target:** preserve a credible path to 1 BSEK+

The value driver is not feature count.

It is whether Handymate becomes a trusted, economically measurable and increasingly intelligent operating system for trade companies.
