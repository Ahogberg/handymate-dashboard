# ImageGen-prompts — Launch 01

Built-in ImageGen användes. Bilderna är konceptuella kampanjmiljöer, inte
kundcase. Text, logotyp och UI genereras aldrig i bildmodellen.

## Arbetsplats — 4:5

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: vertical campaign photography background for a Swedish B2B
software brand, 4:5 composition
Primary request: A premium, natural editorial photograph of a Swedish trades
business owner inside a real Scandinavian renovation site in the early
morning, briefly checking a smartphone while the workday begins. Competent,
calm and busy, not posed like a stock-photo model.
Composition: subject on the right third with generous clean negative space on
the upper-left and left-center for typography.
Palette: slate, off-white, natural wood and restrained teal-compatible tones.
Constraints: no text, UI, logos, watermark, money symbols, robots, holograms
or readable screen. Do not imply a testimonial or real customer.
```

## Servicebil — 9:16

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: vertical 9:16 social video cover/background
Primary request: A photorealistic documentary-style image of a Swedish trades
business owner at an open work van before the first job, checking a smartphone
while organizing the day. Authentic Nordic residential street at dawn.
Composition: subject and van on the right; clean negative space upper-left.
Lighting: cool dawn with a subtle warm practical light inside the van.
Constraints: no text, logo, watermark, readable phone screen, robots, UI,
money symbols or futuristic effects. Conceptual campaign photography only.
```

## Regler för kommande prompts

- Byt yrkesmiljö, ålder och kön mellan bilderna så universumet inte blir en
  och samma generiska manliga byggare.
- Behåll dokumentär realism och svensk/nordisk miljö.
- Ange alltid negativt utrymme för efterföljande typografi.
- Generera aldrig siffror, kundcitat, produkt-UI eller Handymate-logotyp.
- Använd verkliga produktklipp när produkten ska visas.
